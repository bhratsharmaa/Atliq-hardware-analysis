# Atliq-hardware-analysis
# Project Description

Atliq Hardware which sells hardware all over india, is currently facing challenges with its sales performance. The Sales Head is concerned about the recent decline and is seeking insights into the underlying reasons. To address this, the sales team has provided data for analysis.

In this project, we aim to:

- Analyze the data to identify the key reasons behind the decline in sales.
- Provide actionable recommendations to help improve the current sales performance.

The goal is to equip the Sales Head with data-driven insights to make informed decisions and drive growth.

# Steps Performed

1. **Loaded All the Files into Tableau**  
   Imported all the necessary data files into Tableau, ensuring that each dataset was correctly identified and formatted for analysis. This included verifying data types and reviewing initial previews to confirm that the files were ready for integration.

2. **Created a Schema and Established Connections Between Tables**  
   Designed a logical data schema by identifying key relationships among different tables. Established joins and relationships between these tables based on common fields such as Customer ID, Product ID, or Transaction Date to enable a unified and accurate analysis across the datasets.

3. **Performed Data Cleaning**  
   Cleaned the data by handling missing values, correcting data types, and removing any duplicates or inconsistencies. Ensured that the data was reliable and ready for meaningful analysis by resolving issues such as spelling variations, blank fields, and date format mismatches.

4. **Created New Columns for Analysis**  
   Added new calculated fields in Tableau to enhance the analysis. For example, created a “Normalized Currency” column to convert all monetary values into a common currency—Indian Rupees. This was particularly important as some customers had made payments in US Dollars, and a uniform currency was needed for accurate comparison and aggregation.

5. **Developed Informative Charts**  
   Created various charts and visualizations such as bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, and heatmaps to uncover trends, patterns, and insights from the data. Each visualization was designed to answer specific business questions and to support the overall objective of understanding the sales decline.

6. **Built an Interactive Dashboard**  
   Designed and published an interactive dashboard in Tableau that brought all key insights together in a user-friendly format. Incorporated filters, tooltips, and interactive elements to allow stakeholders to explore the data dynamically and derive their own insights based on different parameters like region, product category, or time period.

# Insights

1. **Significant Year-on-Year Revenue Decline**  
   The company has experienced a continuous decline in revenue over the past few years, with a particularly alarming drop of 58% in the current year. This downward trend highlights an urgent need to reassess sales strategies and market engagement.

2. **Delhi and Mumbai Remain Top Performing Markets**  
   Among all regions, Delhi leads in terms of both revenue and quantity sold, followed closely by Mumbai. These cities continue to be key markets and should remain a primary focus for sales and marketing initiatives.

3. **Electrical Sara Stores: Top Revenue-Generating Customer**  
   Electrical Sara Stores stands out as the highest revenue-contributing customer, accounting for approximately 35% of total revenue. This makes them a critical client in the overall business portfolio.

4. **Consistent Dip in Sales During September**  
   An evident seasonal trend shows consistently lower sales figures in the month of September across years. This pattern suggests potential external or operational factors affecting demand during this period, which warrant further investigation.

5. **Declining Purchases from Key Customer**  
   Despite being the largest buyer, Electrical Sara Stores has shown a noticeable year-over-year decrease in purchase volume. This decline is concerning and calls for a deeper analysis into customer satisfaction, pricing, product offerings, or competitor activity.


## Recommendations

1. **Revamp Sales and Marketing Strategy in Underperforming Years**  
   With a steep 58% revenue drop in the current year, it's essential to conduct a root-cause analysis to understand the key drivers—be it market competition, product pricing, or reduced customer engagement. A revised sales strategy, possibly including promotions or discounts, should be introduced to revive momentum.

2. **Strengthen Relationships in High-Performing Regions**  
   Since Delhi and Mumbai continue to contribute the highest sales, focused campaigns, loyalty programs, and exclusive offers in these regions could further boost revenue. Deepening partnerships in these key markets will help stabilize the revenue base.

3. **Re-Engage Electrical Sara Stores with Customized Offers**  
   As the highest revenue-generating customer is showing a decline in purchases, it’s critical to initiate a client retention plan. This could involve direct engagement, understanding their changing needs, offering volume-based incentives, or personalized service to reignite interest.

4. **Address the Seasonal Dip in September Sales**  
   Investigate the recurring drop in sales during September. This could be due to seasonal factors, holidays, or industry trends. Consider running targeted promotional campaigns, new product launches, or bundled deals in August to stimulate demand ahead of the dip.

5. **Introduce Customer Segmentation and Feedback Mechanisms**  
   Implement customer segmentation to tailor offerings based on customer profiles (e.g., large retailers vs. small businesses). Additionally, gather regular feedback to understand pain points, evolving needs, and competitor preferences—helping the sales team act proactively rather than reactively.


## Scope for Further Analysis

- **Include Cost of Production Data**  
  To assess true profitability, we need access to the cost of creation for each product. This will help differentiate between high revenue and high profit, enabling more strategic decisions.

- **Profitability Analysis by State**  
  With cost data, we can determine which states are generating the highest profit margins—not just revenue—and prioritize those regions accordingly.

- **Identify Most Profitable Customers**  
  Analyzing customer-wise profitability will help identify which clients contribute the most to the bottom line, allowing for better-targeted sales efforts and relationship management.

- **Evaluate Cost Optimization Opportunities**  
  Understanding cost structures may uncover areas where expenses can be reduced without compromising product quality, improving overall profitability.

- **Assess Effectiveness of Marketing Campaigns**  
  Further analysis can reveal whether state-wise marketing campaigns are yielding sufficient returns. This includes evaluating whether campaigns are successfully acquiring new customers or increasing sales among existing ones, and if the investment in these campaigns is justified.
